The Novel

No Rescue. No Escape. Not Alone.

Three hundred years from Earth and six hundred years from home, there is no help, no rescue, and nowhere to run.

On board the generation ship Blue Horizon, a child is missing. For Brant Spurgeon, chief Enforcer on the Blue, the case cuts close to home. Determined to track down the abductor and save another family's child as he could not save his own, he must work with the Soothers, technicians and the command crew to find the culprit before the news can trigger civic unrest. But on this ship every human being is constantly monitored, their lives and families controlled by the ship-board computer that protects the Balance. It isn't possible for anyone on board to take a child without being seen. And as the investigation continues, the residents of the Blue will discover the terrifying truth: they are not alone.

Captain Sahara Armytage has responsibility for the lives of twelve thousand crew and civilians, but more important is her duty to a future she will never see. But the taking of a child is only the first action of an enemy that will kill everyone on board rather than face discovery. How much — and who — will she need to sacrifice to preserve her species' future?

The Horizon program was humanity's last desperate chance for survival. But if Brant and Sahara fail, what arrives at the Blue's destination will not be human…

About the Author

Dan Payne

Dan Payne's interests are in science, communications and information management. He has worked as a science communicator, a web programmer, a market research engineer and a librarian. He writes works of magic realism in the genres of science fiction and fantasy with a distinctly Australian edge.

Dan lives in eastern Australia with his wife, one daughter, two cats and more attitude than he knows how to deal with.

When he's not writing, Dan is a musician, painter and amateur prestidigitator.
